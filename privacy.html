Clipper - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

1. Who this applies to

Clipper is a private desktop tool used only by its owner (the operator of @okaensclips) to edit and upload their own video clips. It has no other users and does not collect data from the public.

2. What data Clipper handles

  • Account tokens: when the owner connects their own TikTok account through TikTok's official login, Clipper receives an access token and basic account info (display name, open ID). It never receives the password.
  • Videos: the clips the owner chooses to upload.
  • Post statistics: view, like and comment counts of the owner's own posts, used to improve future edits.

3. How it is stored

All data stays on the owner's own computer. Tokens are stored encrypted with Windows' built-in encryption (DPAPI). Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

4. Data from TikTok

Data obtained through TikTok's APIs is used only to upload the owner's videos and read the owner's own post statistics, in line with TikTok's Developer Terms. It is not shared or transferred to anyone.

5. Deleting data

The owner can disconnect Clipper at any time in the app or in TikTok's settings (Manage app permissions), which revokes access. Removing the app deletes all locally stored data.
